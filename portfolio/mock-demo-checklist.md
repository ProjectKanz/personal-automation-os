# Mock Demo Checklist

Use mock data only when preparing screenshots, clips, or a public walkthrough.

## Safe Demo Data

- Use placeholder user names such as `Portfolio User` or `Demo User`.
- Use fake company names such as `Acme Analytics`, `Northstar Labs`, or `Sample Retail Group`.
- Use fake job titles such as `Data Analyst Intern`, `Business Operations Associate`, or `Automation Analyst`.
- Use sample habit names that do not reveal private routines.
- Use non-private trading examples with small round numbers, or clearly label them as sample data.
- Use a sanitized AI audit that does not mention real companies, CV links, account values, personal issues, or private notes.

## Demo Flow

1. Open the web command center home screen with mock totals.
2. Show the Habit tab and mark one sample habit complete.
3. Show the Career tab with fake applications and update one mock status.
4. Show the Trading tab with sample records and the cumulative chart.
5. Show the AI Audit tab with sanitized weekly recommendations.
6. Optionally show Telegram commands using fake chat content only.

## Do Not Show

- Real CV PDFs or Drive links.
- Real spreadsheet URLs.
- Real Telegram chat IDs, tokens, webhook URLs, or bot setup screens.
- Real company applications or recruiter notes.
- Real trading P/L, account balances, broker details, or screenshots.
- Private habit notes or personal identifiers.
